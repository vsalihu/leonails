# Admin guide

Your dashboard is at **yourdomain/admin**. It works on your phone; add it to your home screen for one-tap access.

## Signing in

Use your email and password. If you forget it, tap **Forgotten your password?** and a one-time link (valid for one hour) is emailed to you. Change your password any time in **Settings > Account**.

## Every day: Today

* Your appointments for today, the next client (tap their number to call), and gaps you could fill.
* **Attention** notes appear when something needs you: emails that failed, past appointments still marked "confirmed", bookings affected by a change to your hours, reviews to approve, new enquiries.
* **New booking** to add a client yourself; **Block time** for appointments of your own.

## After each appointment

Open the booking (from Today or Bookings) and:

1. **Record payment:** amount and method (cash, card, bank transfer). The expected amount is shown separately so you can spot differences.
2. **After the appointment:** mark **Completed** (tick "email a review invitation" if you'd like a review) or **No-show**.

If you set the wrong status, choose the right one and give a short reason; it's kept in the booking's history.

## Changing or cancelling a booking

On the booking page:

* **Move appointment:** pick a new date and time. If it overlaps another appointment, it's refused and nothing changes. Inside the 24-hour cutoff you need to tick *override* and give a reason. If an offer only applied to certain dates, you'll be asked to accept the new price.
* **Cancel appointment:** add a reason, choose whether to email the client. The time becomes free straight away and their reminder is stopped.
* **Re-send confirmation:** emails the client a fresh copy with a new private link.
* **Revoke customer links:** if a client's link has been shared with the wrong person.

Clients can also move or cancel online themselves, up to 24 hours before (you can change this in **Settings > Booking rules**). You get an email when they do (set your address in **Settings > Business**).

## Calendar

Week view shows your working hours in white, appointments as cards, and dashed boxes for clients in the middle of booking. Tap a day for the day view and its free gaps (with a **Book** shortcut).

**Block time or change a day** at the bottom:

* *Block a period:* e.g. dentist 14:00 to 15:00.
* *Close the whole day:* holidays.
* *Set different opening hours:* e.g. late opening before Christmas.

Existing appointments are never moved or cancelled by these changes. If one ends up outside your hours, it's flagged on Today for you to sort out with the client.

## Customers

Search by name, email or phone. Each profile shows their appointments, offers used, and **private notes** (allergies, preferences) that clients never see. You can **block online booking** for someone who repeatedly doesn't turn up; give a reason. Their existing appointments stay as they are.

If two profiles share a phone number, it's pointed out but they're never merged automatically.

## Treatments

Tap a treatment to edit its name, price, length, description and compatible extras. Use the arrows to reorder. **Hidden** removes it from booking; **Archived** removes it from lists too. Existing bookings always keep the price and length they were booked with. If you change a length, any upcoming bookings for that treatment are flagged for you to check.

## Promotions

Create codes (e.g. `WELCOME20`) or automatic offers:

* percentage or fixed amount, optional maximum saving and minimum spend;
* first visit only, or everyone;
* dates it can be used, and dates of appointments it covers;
* total uses and uses per client;
* optional banner on the website.

Only one offer applies per booking: the client gets whichever saves them most. The list shows how many times each offer has been used.

## Gallery

**Upload images** (several at once). Location data is removed from photos automatically. For each image:

* add a short **description** (needed before publishing, for people using screen readers) and an optional caption;
* choose a **style** (French, nude, nail art, occasion) for the gallery filters;
* click on the photo to set the **crop focus**, the part that must stay in view;
* tick **Published** and optionally **Feature on the homepage**.

**Website images** (top of the gallery page) are the fixed pictures on the homepage and About page. Use *Use on website as* to place them.

**Homepage video.** Under Website images you can upload a short MP4 or WebM video (up to 40 MB; about 8 to 10 seconds at 1080p, exported for web, works best). Add a description, choose *Use on website as: Homepage hero video* and tick Published. It plays silently on a loop beside the headline, with a pause button. The homepage hero *image* is used as its first frame and for visitors who have turned off motion on their device, so keep a good hero image set too. To go back to the still image, choose *Stop using as homepage hero video*.

## Reviews

Reviews clients send through their invitation link wait here until you **approve** them (with or without their photo). You can **feature**, **hide**, **reject** or **delete** any review. **Invite recent clients** lists completed appointments you haven't asked yet. **Add a review you received elsewhere** (e.g. a message) with a note of how they agreed to it being published.

## Messages

* **Enquiries** from the contact form. Reply by email and mark them handled.
* **Email delivery** shows every email the site has sent or tried to send. **Failed** ones show the reason and a **Retry** button.
* **Development mailbox** only matters before email is connected: it holds emails that were *not* sent.

## Settings

| Section | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| Business | Name, town shown publicly, public contact details, and where your booking alerts go |
| Booking rules | Start-time spacing, buffer between clients, minimum notice, how far ahead clients can book, change/cancel cutoff, reminder timing, and a switch to pause online booking |
| Working hours | Your normal week, with an optional break each day |
| Private address | The address only confirmed clients see, plus arrival instructions |
| Website copy | Headlines and text on the homepage, About and Treatments pages |
| Policies | Cancellation, booking terms, privacy. Saving creates a new version; each booking remembers which version the client agreed to |
| System | Whether email, image storage and background sending are working |
| Activity log | Who changed what, and when |

## Things that are marked "Example"

Anything labelled **Example** (prices, hours, address, images, reviews, policies) was put in for setup and must be replaced or removed before the site goes public. Today shows a **Before launch** list until everything is real.
